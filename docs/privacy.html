Arc Favorites+ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Arc Favorites+ is a browser extension and companion menu bar app that lets you save more than Arc's built-in 12-favorite limit. This page explains exactly what data it touches and where it goes.

The short version: the only data this extension handles is the URL and title of a page you deliberately right-click to save. That data goes to one place — a small server the companion app runs on your own Mac (127.0.0.1) — and never leaves your computer. Nothing is sent to us, to any analytics service, or to any third party.

What data is collected

  • Page URL and title — only for pages you explicitly right-click and choose "Add to Arc Favorites+." Nothing is captured automatically or in the background.
  • A pairing token — a random identifier you copy from the companion app and paste into the extension's settings, used only to authenticate the extension to your own local app instance.

The extension does not collect browsing history, keystrokes, form data, passwords, or any content from pages you haven't explicitly saved.

Where the data goes

Saved URLs, titles, and the pairing token are sent only to http://127.0.0.1:8765 — a local server that the Arc Favorites+ menu bar app runs on your own machine. This address is only reachable from your own computer; it is not a remote server, and no data crosses the network to anywhere else.

The saved favorites themselves are stored in a local file on your Mac (inside your user Library folder) by the companion app. We — the developer — never receive, see, or have access to this data in any form.

What the extension's permissions are for

  • contextMenus — adds the "Add to Arc Favorites+" right-click menu item.
  • activeTab — reads the URL/title of the current tab only when you invoke the menu item, never in the background.
  • storage — stores your pairing token and a small retry queue locally in the browser, using Chrome's own extension storage.
  • alarms — periodically retries sending a saved favorite to the local app if it wasn't running at the time (e.g. you saved a page before opening the app).
  • notifications — shows a native notification confirming a favorite was saved, or explaining why it wasn't (e.g. pairing needed, free-tier limit reached).
  • host_permissions (http://127.0.0.1/*) — the only network destination this extension ever talks to: the companion app running on your own machine.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. There is no analytics or tracking of any kind in this extension.

Data retention and deletion

Saved favorites and the pairing token live entirely on your own device. Uninstalling the extension removes its local Chrome storage; uninstalling the companion app (or running brew uninstall --cask arcfavoritesplus --zap) removes the favorites file and pairing token from your Mac.
